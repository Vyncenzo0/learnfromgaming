What is RJ45?

RJ45 (Registered Jack 45) is a standard connector used for Ethernet networking. It has 8 pins and uses twisted pair cables (Cat5e, Cat6, etc.).

Crimping Tips 💡

• Strip about 1 inch of outer jacket.
• Untwist pairs and arrange according to standard.
• Cut wires evenly before inserting into connector.
• Push wires all the way to the end of the connector.
• Crimp firmly and test with a LAN tester.
